SentinelGrid Defense — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 27, 2026  |  Package: com.sentinelgrid.defense  |  Contact: bmwm25320@gmail.com

SentinelGrid Defense is an endpoint security, mobile antivirus, and privacy utility built for transparent local device defense.

1. Permissions & On-Device Processing

  • QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES: Used exclusively on-device for the core Antivirus & Stalkerware Scanner to inspect installed applications for sideloaded APKs, overlay + SMS trojan combinations, and hidden surveillance packages. App lists are never sold or uploaded to ad networks.
  • BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE: Optional user-enabled Safe Messaging Shield that inspects URLs inside notifications locally to warn against phishing links. Message bodies are never transmitted off-device.
  • PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS & USE_BIOMETRIC: Optional user-enabled Biometric App Lock enforcing local fingerprint/PIN checks on user-selected apps.
  • SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW: Optional floating Always-On Gemini Co-Pilot overlay toggled by the user.
  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION: Accessed only when the user manually triggers Anti-Theft GPS Location.

2. Authentication, Vault & AI Assistant

  • Google Sign-In: Powered by Firebase Authentication to identify your operator session.
  • Password Vault & Breach Checks: Credentials remain encrypted locally behind your Dedicated PIN; Dark Web checks send only a 5-character SHA-1 hash prefix (k-anonymity) to Have I Been Pwned.
  • Gemini AI Assistant: Processes only the security queries you explicitly ask.
